## Description
Everything about your rental property, organised and documented in one place.

HomeLedger is for landlords and tenants. Use it on your own, or link up with the other side when you’re ready.

INSPECTIONS YOU CAN RELY ON
• Guided room-by-room ingoing, routine and outgoing inspections with detailed checklists
• Mark each item good, defective, not tested or not applicable: nothing unchecked is ever assumed to be fine
• Photos and videos attached to the exact room and item, kept as the original files
• Server upload times shown separately from camera times, with file fingerprints for every photo
• Completed inspections are locked; both sides can sign, agree or dispute specific findings
• Professional PDF reports to save or share

FOR LANDLORDS
• Manage one property or a whole portfolio
• Leases with escalations, deposits and a month-by-month rent schedule
• Occupancy, upcoming lease expiries and escalations at a glance
• Private ownership and finance details your tenants never see

FOR TENANTS
• Document the home you rent from day one, even if your landlord doesn’t use the app
• Keep your evidence and reports after you move out
• Your previous tenancies are never shared with new landlords

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
• Your records are yours; people see only what you share with them
• Data stored in South Africa (Johannesburg)
• Export your data or delete your account at any time

## Apple App Privacy (“Data Used to Track You”: none)
Data **linked to the user**, purpose **App Functionality** only, **not used for tracking**:
- Contact Info → **Name**, **Email Address**
- User Content → **Photos or Videos**, **Other User Content** (inspection notes, signatures, property and lease records)
- Identifiers → **User ID**

Not collected: location (we don’t read photo GPS; original files may contain it if the camera wrote it, see privacy policy), financial info (amounts are typed by the user as record content, not payment data), contacts, browsing, usage data, diagnostics, advertising data.
