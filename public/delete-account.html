Delete your HomeLedger account

In the app (fastest)

  1. Open HomeLedger and sign in.
  2. Go to Settings.
  3. Choose Export my data first if you want a copy.
  4. Choose Delete my account, confirm with your password and type DELETE.

Deletion happens straight away.

By email

If you can’t use the app, email memmamally@gmail.com from the email address on your account with the subject “Delete my HomeLedger account”. We’ll confirm it’s you and delete the account within 30 days.

What is deleted, and what is kept

  • Deleted: your profile and sign-in, invitations you sent, properties only you are on, inspections only you can see, and their photos and records.
  • Kept for others: inspections another person took part in stay with them, because they are their records too, with your name on what you submitted. A property you co-own passes to your co-owner.
  • Information may be kept longer where the law requires it.
