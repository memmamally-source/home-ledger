Help and support

Email memmamally@gmail.com and we’ll reply as soon as we can. Include the email address you use in the app and, if something went wrong, a screenshot.

Getting started. Choose whether you’re a landlord, a tenant or both, then add a property. Tenants can document their home without their landlord using the app.
Invitations. To link with a landlord or tenant, open a property, go to People and choose Invite. Send the link yourself. In the mobile app, use “Join with a link” to paste an invitation you received.
Inspections and photos. Photos taken without a connection are kept on your device and upload automatically when you reconnect. Keep the app open until the upload counter clears.
Reports. Open a completed inspection and choose Report to download or share the PDF.
Your data and privacy. Read the privacy policy. Export your data or delete your account in Settings, or see how to delete your account.
