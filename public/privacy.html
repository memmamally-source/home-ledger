HomeLedger

Privacy policy

Effective 30 October 2026 · Draft for the test version, to be reviewed before public launch

HomeLedger helps landlords and tenants keep organised records of a rental property: the property, the lease, inspections with photos, and signatures. This policy explains what personal information the app processes, why, where it is kept and what you can do about it. We have written it with South Africa’s Protection of Personal Information Act (POPIA) in mind.

Who is responsible

HomeLedger is operated by its developer in South Africa (“we”). Contact us at memmamally@gmail.com for any privacy question or request.

What we process

  • Account details: your name, email address and sign-in method; your country and display preferences.
  • Property and tenancy records you enter: addresses, lease terms, rent and deposit amounts, names and emails of tenants or landlords you record, and for owners, private ownership and finance details.
  • Inspections: checklists, conditions, comments, signatures and acknowledgements.
  • Photos and videos you attach, stored as the original files. Original files can contain metadata written by your camera, such as the time taken, the camera model and, if your camera recorded it, location. We read the capture time and camera model to show them next to the photo, and we record only whether location data is present.
  • Integrity and activity records: server times, file fingerprints (hashes) and an activity log of actions on a property, so records can be relied on.
  • Technical data: our hosting provider processes IP addresses and similar data to run sign-in and keep the service secure.

We do not use advertising, we do not track you across other apps or websites, and we do not sell personal information.

Why

To provide the service you asked for: keeping your records, sharing them with the people you choose, producing reports, and protecting the integrity of evidence. We rely on performing our agreement with you and on our legitimate interest in keeping records accurate and secure.

Who can see your information

  • Your records are private to you unless you share them. People you invite to a property see only what their role allows: tenants never see an owner’s private finance details.
  • When you share an inspection with someone, it becomes part of their records too. They keep access even if you later leave the property or delete your account.
  • We never share your previous tenancies with new landlords.
  • Service provider: Google Firebase (Google Cloud) hosts the app. Your database and files are stored in Google’s Johannesburg region (africa-south1). Google’s sign-in service (Firebase Authentication) may process account details outside South Africa under Google’s data protection terms.

How long we keep it

Until you delete it or delete your account. Records shared with other people are kept for them as described below. We may keep information longer where the law requires it.

Your rights

  • Access and a copy: Settings → Export my data downloads everything you can access.
  • Correction: edit your profile and records in the app, or contact us.
  • Deletion: Settings → Delete my account, or see how to delete your account.
  • Objection and complaints: contact us first. You may also complain to the Information Regulator (South Africa): inforegulator.org.za.

Deleting your account

Deleting your account removes your profile, sign-in, invitations, the properties only you are on and the inspections only you can see, including photos. Inspections other people took part in remain with them, because they are their records too, and your name stays on what you submitted there. A property you co-own passes to your co-owner.

Security

Data is encrypted in transit and at rest by our hosting provider. Access is enforced by security rules on the server, photos are never shared through public links, and evidence, signatures and activity records cannot be altered after they are submitted.

Children

HomeLedger is for adults managing rental property and is not intended for anyone under 18.

Changes

We will update this page when the policy changes and tell you in the app about significant changes.
